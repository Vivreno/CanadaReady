Exam Day Guide

📋

What to Bring

Your Notice to Appear, a valid photo ID, and permanent resident card. Arrive 15 minutes early. The test is usually written (20 questions, 30 minutes) but can be an oral interview.

✅

Passing Score

You need to answer at least 15 out of 20 questions correctly (75%) to pass. If you fail, you will be invited for an interview with a citizenship officer.

🌐

Language Requirement

Applicants between 18–54 must demonstrate adequate knowledge of English or French. Adults 55+ are exempt from the language requirement AND the written test.

📌

Most Tested Topics

Rights & responsibilities, how Parliament works, voting procedures, Canadian history milestones (Confederation 1867, WWII, Charter 1982), provinces & capitals, and key symbols.

🏛️

After Passing

You'll receive a Notice to Appear for the citizenship ceremony. At the ceremony: take the Oath of Citizenship, sign the oath form, and receive your Canadian Citizenship Certificate.

📞

Need Help?

Call IRCC at 1-888-242-2100 (toll-free in Canada). Visit www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship for the official Discover Canada guide, free language classes, and application tracking.

🧠

Study Strategy

Complete all 9 lessons, do the flashcards until you get 80%+ "Got it" marks, then take at least 3 full practice quizzes. Focus extra time on dates, names of Prime Ministers on bills, and the structure of Parliament.

🍁

Key Oath Pledge

You will swear allegiance to the Sovereign (the King or Queen of Canada), promise to faithfully observe the laws of Canada including the Constitution, and fulfil your duties as a Canadian citizen.
